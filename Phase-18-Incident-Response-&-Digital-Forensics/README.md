# Phase 18 — Incident Response & Digital Forensics

## Objective
Learn the stages of incident response and the importance of preserving evidence during a security investigation.

## Topics Covered
- Preparation
- Detection and analysis
- Containment
- Eradication and recovery
- Lessons learned
- Evidence integrity
- Documentation and timelines

## Activities
- Draft a basic incident-response checklist.
- Build a timeline from a hypothetical or authorized lab event.
- Record the source of each observation and the time it was collected.
- Preserve original evidence and work from copies where possible.
- Avoid making destructive changes before evidence has been considered.

## Analysis and Findings
- Earlier lab troubleshooting demonstrates why recording commands, symptoms, and changes helps explain how a problem was investigated.
- Packet captures and system logs can provide complementary evidence, but neither should be interpreted without context.

## What I Learned
Incident response is a structured process, not just fixing a computer. Careful documentation, evidence preservation, and clear communication help teams make defensible decisions.

## Cybersecurity Relevance
These skills are important for SOC analysts, incident responders, and digital-forensics practitioners.
